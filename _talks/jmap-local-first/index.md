## Abstract

"It's a new way of communicating, and it's going to kill email!" I've heard it
before. You've heard it before. It hasn't really happened. It's easy to see why
you'd want it to die, though — it's made of ick, and you've got to learn too
many protocols from the 80s to get anything done. Honestly, though, if you can
get past the stink, email has long been a local-first system. JMAP is the
stink-free, local-first email system — and it's not just for email, either!
